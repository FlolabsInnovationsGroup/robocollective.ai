<h2>RM75-6F Robotic Arm: 7-DOF Force-Sensing Precision Manipulator</h2>
<p>The <strong>RM75-6F</strong>, a 6-axis-force version of the 7-DOF robotic arm, is self-developed and produced by RealMan. The robotic arm integrating the controller weighs <strong>7.9 kg</strong> in total, with a payload of <strong>5 kg</strong> and a working radius of <strong>638.5 mm</strong>.</p>

<h3>Extended Dexterity with Force Sensing</h3>
<p>With <strong>7 degrees of freedom</strong> and integrated <strong>six-axis force sensing (200 N / 7 Nm)</strong>, the RM75-6F offers enhanced maneuverability for complex manipulation tasks. Force accuracy of <strong>&lt;0.1% FS</strong> enables delicate handling in assembly, research, and quality control applications.</p>

<h3>Compact and Durable Build</h3>
<p>Built from <strong>aluminum alloy</strong> with an <strong>IP54-rated body</strong>, the RM75-6F is designed for reliable operation in industrial and laboratory settings. Its integrated controller simplifies installation, and the arm runs on <strong>DC 24V</strong> with power consumption up to <strong>200 W</strong>.</p>

<h3>Flexible Control and Connectivity</h3>
<p>The RM75-6F supports <strong>Wi-Fi, network port, Bluetooth, USB serial port, and RS485</strong> communication. Programming options include <strong>drag-and-drop teaching, teaching pendant, API, and JSON</strong> control modes for flexible deployment.</p>

<h3>Motion Performance</h3>
<p>Seven joints provide an extended motion envelope, with ranges from <strong>±128° to ±360°</strong> and maximum speeds of <strong>180°/s (J1-J2)</strong> and <strong>225°/s (J3-J7)</strong>. The extra degree of freedom enables smoother trajectories and access to confined workspaces.</p>
