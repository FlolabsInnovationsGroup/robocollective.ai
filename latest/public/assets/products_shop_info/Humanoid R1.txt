<h2>Unitree R1 Humanoid Robot</h2>
<p>The <strong>Unitree R1</strong> is a compact, versatile humanoid platform for manipulation tasks and real-world deployment in structured environments. Its lightweight 25 kg frame, 8-core high-performance CPU, onboard speaker and microphone, and Wi-Fi/Bluetooth connectivity make it ready for research, education, and practical demonstrations.</p>

<h3>Specifications</h3>
<ul>
 	<li>Dimensions: 1210 x 357 x 190 mm</li>
 	<li>Net weight: 25 kg</li>
 	<li>Power supply: Lithium battery</li>
 	<li>Computing power: 8-core high-performance CPU</li>
 	<li>Speaker/Microphone: Yes</li>
 	<li>Wi-Fi/Bluetooth: Yes</li>
 	<li>Battery endurance: up to 1 h</li>
