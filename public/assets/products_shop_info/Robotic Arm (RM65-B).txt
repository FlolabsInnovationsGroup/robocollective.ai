<h2>RM65-B Robotic Arm: Versatile 6-DOF Automation Platform</h2>
<p>The <strong>RM65-B</strong>, a basic version of the 6-DOF robotic arm, is self-developed and produced by RealMan. The robotic arm integrating the controller weighs <strong>7.2 kg</strong> in total, with a payload of <strong>5 kg</strong>, a peak load of <strong>9 kg</strong>, and a working radius of <strong>610 mm</strong>.</p>

<h3>Precision and Payload</h3>
<p>With <strong>±0.05 mm repeatability</strong> and a <strong>5 kg payload</strong>, the RM65-B delivers consistent results for assembly, pick-and-place, and light industrial tasks. Its compact footprint and integrated controller make it easy to deploy in labs, production lines, and research environments.</p>

<h3>Compact and Durable Build</h3>
<p>Built from <strong>aluminum alloy</strong> with an <strong>IP54-rated body</strong>, the RM65-B is protected against dust and splashes. It runs on <strong>DC 24V</strong> with a maximum power consumption of <strong>200 W</strong> and a comprehensive consumption of <strong>100 W</strong>, keeping operating costs low.</p>

<h3>Flexible Control and Connectivity</h3>
<p>The RM65-B supports <strong>Wi-Fi, network port, Bluetooth, USB serial port, and RS485</strong> communication. Operators can program the arm using <strong>drag-and-drop teaching, teaching pendant, API, and JSON</strong> control modes for fast setup and seamless integration.</p>

<h3>Motion Performance</h3>
<p>With joint motion ranges from <strong>±128° to ±360°</strong> across all six axes and maximum speeds of <strong>180°/s (J1-J2)</strong> and <strong>225°/s (J3-J6)</strong>, the RM65-B offers responsive, accurate motion for a wide range of automation applications.</p>
