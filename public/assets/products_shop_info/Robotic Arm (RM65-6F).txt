<h2>RM65-6F Robotic Arm: 6-Axis Force-Sensing 6-DOF Precision</h2>
<p>The <strong>RM65-6F</strong>, a 6-axis-force version of the 6-DOF robotic arm, is self-developed and produced by RealMan. The robotic arm integrating the controller weighs <strong>7.3 kg</strong> in total, with a payload of <strong>5 kg</strong>, a peak load of <strong>9 kg</strong>, and a working radius of <strong>638.5 mm</strong>.</p>

<h3>Force Sensing and Precision</h3>
<p>Equipped with integrated <strong>six-axis force sensing</strong>, the RM65-6F delivers a force range of <strong>200 N / 7 Nm</strong> with accuracy better than <strong>&lt;0.1% FS</strong>. Combined with <strong>±0.05 mm repeatability</strong>, it excels at delicate assembly, quality inspection, and tasks requiring tactile feedback.</p>

<h3>Compact and Durable Build</h3>
<p>Built from <strong>aluminum alloy</strong> with an <strong>IP54-rated body</strong>, the RM65-6F withstands industrial environments while remaining lightweight. Its integrated controller simplifies deployment, and the arm runs on <strong>DC 24V</strong> with a maximum power consumption of <strong>200 W</strong>.</p>

<h3>Flexible Control and Connectivity</h3>
<p>The RM65-6F supports <strong>Wi-Fi, network port, Bluetooth, USB serial port, and RS485</strong> communication. Programming is available through <strong>drag-and-drop teaching, teaching pendant, API, and JSON</strong> control modes, making integration straightforward for both operators and developers.</p>

<h3>Motion Performance</h3>
<p>With joint motion ranges from <strong>±128° to ±360°</strong> across all six axes and maximum speeds of <strong>180°/s (J1–J2)</strong> and <strong>225°/s (J3–J6)</strong>, the RM65-6F balances speed and accuracy for demanding automation workflows.</p>
