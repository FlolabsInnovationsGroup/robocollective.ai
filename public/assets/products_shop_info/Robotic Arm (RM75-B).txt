<h2>RM75-B Robotic Arm: 7-DOF Versatile Automation Platform</h2>
<p>The <strong>RM75-B</strong>, a basic version of the 7-DOF robotic arm, is self-developed and produced by RealMan. The robotic arm integrating the controller weighs <strong>7.8 kg</strong> in total, with a payload of <strong>5 kg</strong>, a peak load of <strong>9 kg</strong>, and a working radius of <strong>610 mm</strong>.</p>

<h3>Extended Reach and Precision</h3>
<p>With <strong>7 degrees of freedom</strong> and <strong>±0.05 mm repeatability</strong>, the RM75-B delivers enhanced dexterity for complex manipulation tasks. Its <strong>5 kg payload</strong> and <strong>610 mm working radius</strong> make it suitable for assembly, research, and flexible automation workflows.</p>

<h3>Compact and Durable Build</h3>
<p>Built from <strong>aluminum alloy</strong> with an <strong>IP54-rated body</strong>, the RM75-B is protected against dust and splashes in demanding environments. The integrated controller streamlines setup, and the arm operates on <strong>DC 24V</strong> with a maximum power consumption of <strong>200 W</strong>.</p>

<h3>Flexible Control and Connectivity</h3>
<p>The RM75-B supports <strong>Wi-Fi, network port, Bluetooth, USB serial port, and RS485</strong> communication. Operators can program the arm using <strong>drag-and-drop teaching, teaching pendant, API, and JSON</strong> control modes for fast integration into existing systems.</p>

<h3>Motion Performance</h3>
<p>Seven joints provide an extended motion envelope, with ranges from <strong>±128° to ±360°</strong> and maximum speeds of <strong>180°/s (J1-J2)</strong> and <strong>225°/s (J3-J7)</strong>. The additional degree of freedom enables smoother paths and improved access to tight workspaces.</p>
